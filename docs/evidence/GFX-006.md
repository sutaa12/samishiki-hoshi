## Telemetry evidence

nearest-rank P50/P95/P99. Dropped RAF intervals remain in the window;
main-work/GPU values are recorded only for submitted frames. Exact upload and
activation frames are excluded from steady-state percentiles. Initialization,
compile, upload, activation, quality changes, and every history-reset reason
remain independently observable. Event storage is capped at 1,024 entries.
