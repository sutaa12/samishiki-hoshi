## Delivered behavior

- The production camera follows the canonical Flow spline using canonical
  distance and corridor offset, with 0.75 seconds of look-ahead and distinct
  near/mid/far parallax factors.
- Reduced motion preserves forward travel while suppressing roll, sway, and
  corridor response.
- A persistent Phase Director blends background, fog, ambient light, sun,
  exposure, material, particle, and audio-layer parameters across the centered
  eight-second phase window.
- Journey-wide phase progress remains continuous while a separate local mix
  controls adjacent-phase interpolation. Exposure is applied to the renderer,
  material to prewarmed TSL uniforms, particle to a persistent visual layer,
  and audio-layer to a production-only Web Audio presentation graph.
- Four fixed GPU chunk slots remain the resident
  behind/current/ahead-1/ahead-2 window and are placed on the same Flow rail.
- Presentation projection normalizes signed zero without changing canonical
  simulation state, replay input, TwinkleSeed ordering, or hashes.
- The supplemental phase-audio graph now retains local ownership until publish.
  Every partial graph rolls back through reverse oscillator stop, reverse node
  disconnect, and context close. Cleanup failures are accumulated without
  replacing the original construction error, and disposal is Promise-idempotent.

still-pending reference-hardware gate. A stale foundation scene-child assertion
was updated from five to six for the Phase Director light. The inherited
cumulative 600 draw-call ceiling is retained, with an additional normalized

observed 154-189 cumulative frames and exceeded 600 by 5-8 calls even though
normalized render density remained at or below 3.52 calls/frame. An isolated
density rerun passed. The final QA evidence now latches the first renderer
