## Contract proven

- `RenderHost` owns one frame loop and the complete initialization, ready,
  failure, disposal, and idle-barrier lifecycle.
- Initialization, control operations, frames, failure cleanup, and disposal are
  serialized. Reentrant or post-terminal operations fail closed.

  failing, and repeated disposal paths release features, materials, uploads,
  registry, backend, listeners, frame relay, and resize relay.

  as one terminal graph. Raw provenance and intermediate safe snapshots report
  zero retained ownership after finalization.

  mode, resource provenance, renderer disposal, and backend disposal remain
  separate facts.
- Forced WebGL2 and host Apple Metal actual WebGPU each initialize, precompile,
  render, dispose, and recreate using a fresh canvas ownership unit.
- Late device loss leaves `ready`, surfaces a structured terminal error, waits
  for owned work, and releases all observable application ownership.
