## Observations and limits

- Unsafe SwiftShader WebGPU on this toolchain reproduced invalid Texture/TextureView/CommandBuffer validation events followed by device loss. It is `WEBGPU_SWIFTSHADER_BLOCKED`, not fallback acceptance. This resembles an open Three.js CI investigation targeted at r186: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/issues/33559.
- The accepted lab path is host Apple Metal WebGPU plus forced SwiftShader WebGL2. It is not a High-tier, reference-device, or performance claim.
- Single-run init/compile/render durations are diagnostics only. Notion P50/P95/P99 and GPU performance gates remain pending.
- GFX-001 proves one runtime can dispose cleanly and idempotently; it does not claim the R2-G6 ten-restart monotonic-growth gate.
- The v1 `ShaderMaterial` implementation is incompatible with `WebGPURenderer`; the spike imports no v1 world/visual asset.
- Reference-hardware performance remains `HARDWARE_PENDING`; `main`, the saved Sites version, and production deployment remain frozen.
