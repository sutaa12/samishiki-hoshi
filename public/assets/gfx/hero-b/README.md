# Hero B natural-surface textures

These three static textures raise the material frequency of the procedural Hero B meshes. They do not replace the interactive 3D scene and they add no runtime AI or paid API dependency.

| Asset | Purpose | Built-in ImageGen source | Conversion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `wet-basalt-v1.webp` | wet waterfall cliff and riverbank rocks | `exec-490f3e7c-63df-45bb-92da-c6c105ca81d2.png` | `cwebp -q 88 -resize 1024 1024` |
| `forest-foliage-v1.webp` | tree crowns, grass, moss, and rooftop foliage | `exec-3b1f1557-0e79-4fab-9fb1-30b94f72c6ef.png` | `cwebp -q 88 -resize 1024 1024` |
| `dark-bark-v1.webp` | trunks and branches | `exec-2570b157-2a74-4fa3-94eb-fda1095b57eb.png` | `cwebp -q 88 -resize 1024 1024` |
| `leaf-cluster-v1.webp` | alpha-tested crossed cards around procedural canopy cores | `exec-3d4a6893-4c53-4e5a-b123-83067537eb0a.png` | `cwebp -q 90 -alpha_q 95 -resize 1024 1024` |
| `waterfall-flow-v1.webp` | translucent broken-flow detail on the layered waterfall ribbons | `exec-45c44b43-aac4-4a4d-83ed-6832199812e1.png` | `cwebp -q 90 -alpha_q 95 -resize 1024 1024` |

The surface prompts requested seamless, orthographic, neutral-diffuse photoreal natural-surface scans with no text, watermark, landscape, focal object, hard shadow, or baked highlight. The basalt prompt specified dark wet slate, fissures, moss, lichen, and restrained vertical wet streaks. The foliage prompt specified mixed temperate broadleaf, fern, and moss microstructure. The bark prompt specified mature dark bark with fine vertical ridges, gray weathering, and sparse moss. The leaf-card prompt requested one asymmetric temperate leaf cluster with genuine transparent alpha, natural silhouette holes, neutral forest light, and no trunk, ground, border, or shadow. The waterfall prompt requested seamless vertical overlapping water streams with aeration detail and genuine transparent gaps, without cliff, pool, cyan glow, or a solid rectangular background.

These are candidate art assets. Human art acceptance and rights acceptance remain separate pending gates under D-005.
